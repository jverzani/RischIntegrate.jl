    How it works#

    The integrand is parsed into an exact differential field and integrated there by the Risch algorithm, following Bronstein, Symbolic Integration I. The answer is checked by differentiation in the same field, then rebuilt as a term of the user's package.

    The steps#

    1. Rational functions \(ℚ(x)\): Hermite reduction (Bronstein §2.2) gives the rational part, the Rothstein–Trager resultant the logarithmic part. Quadratic residues give log and atan terms over \(ℚ(\sqrt d)\) (with Rioboo's LogToAtan for real, continuous atan terms); quartic residues are written with real radicals when possible; other residues of degree greater than 2 give a RootSum.

    2. Towers \(ℚ(x)(θ_1)…(θ_n)\): exp, log and atan are monomials over the field below them; sin, cos, tan, … are rewritten with complex exponentials over \(ℚ(i)\). The structure theorems (Bronstein ch. 9) decide whether a new exp or log is algebraically independent of the previous ones.

    3. Integration in a monomial extension (Bronstein ch. 5): Hermite reduction with the tower's derivation, the residue criterion for the logarithmic part, and the polynomial part through the Risch differential equation \(y' + f y = g\) (ch. 6) and its parametric form (ch. 7). When there is no solution, the integral is proved to be non-elementary (NotElementary).

    4. Real forms: answers over \(ℚ(i)\) are written as real terms, continuous on the real line where the integrand is (Bioche's rules choose simpler substitutions for trigonometric integrands).

    5. Constants: the constant field can be \(ℚ\), \(ℚ(i)\), \(ℚ(\sqrt d)\) or a field of symbolic parameters, e and logarithms of numbers.

    6. Substitutions reduce other integrands to these cases: radicals of a linear-fractional radicand, Euler substitutions for square roots of quadratics, asin/acos by \(u = \sin t\), and u-substitution (derivative-divides).

    7. Special functions: non-elementary integrals of the forms \(∫ e^{u}/v\), \(∫ e^{-x^2}\), \(∫ \sin(x)/x\), \(∫ \log(x)/(x - a)\), … are written with expinti, erf, erfi, sinint, cosint and li2.

    The source files#

    The sources in src/ are included by src/RischIntegrate.jl in this order:

    File Contents
    core.jl number and constant types, the error types
    polynomials.jl dense univariate polynomials: Euclid, extended gcd, squarefree factorisation, resultant
    fractions.jl the fraction field of a polynomial ring
    factoring.jl integer factoring, rational roots, factorisation over \(ℚ\) (Cantor–Zassenhaus)
    number_fields.jl \(ℚ(i)\) and \(B(\sqrt d)\)
    hermite_reduction.jl Hermite reduction
    rothstein_trager.jl the logarithmic part of a rational function
    log_to_atan.jl Rioboo's LogToAtan
    quartic_residues.jl real forms of quartic residues, nested radicals
    term_input.jl, term_output.jl from terms to exact elements and back
    tower.jl towers of monomials and their derivation
    constant_fields.jl the constant fields
    structure_theorems.jl exp and log of tower elements (Risch's structure theorems)
    tower_terms.jl building the tower of an integrand
    rde_base.jl, risch_de.jl the Risch differential equation
    parametric_rde.jl the parametric Risch differential equation
    monomial_integration.jl integration in a monomial extension
    dilogarithms.jl dilogarithms
    exp_trig_polynomials.jl polynomials times exponentials and trigonometric functions
    special_functions.jl non-elementary integrals with special functions
    real_form.jl real forms of answers over \(ℚ(i)\)
    radicals.jl, euler_substitutions.jl radicals by substitution
    rootsum.jl, tower_rootsums.jl, tower_real_forms.jl, rootsum_numerics.jl RootSums: construction, real forms, numeric evaluation
    bioche.jl Bioche's rules
    sturm.jl real roots and signs (Sturm sequences)
    inverse_trig.jl asin, acos, acot
    u_substitution.jl u-substitution
    risch_integrate.jl the entry point
    real_log_arguments.jl real arguments of logarithms in substitution answers
    precompile.jl the precompile workload
